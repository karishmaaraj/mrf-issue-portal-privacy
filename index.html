Privacy Policy

MRF Issue Portal

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the MRF Issue Portal may collect, use, and protect information when users submit and manage issues through the portal or its connected WhatsApp notification service.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how the service is used, information may include names, contact details, issue descriptions, ticket identifiers, ticket status, assigned worker details, notes, and dates associated with an issue.

When WhatsApp messaging is used, we may also process WhatsApp phone numbers, message content sent to the service, and delivery or message status information provided by Meta.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to receive and manage reported issues, assign work, communicate ticket updates, send relevant notifications, maintain service security, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be made available to authorized personnel who need it to manage or resolve an issue. WhatsApp communications are processed through Meta's services when that integration is enabled. We do not intend to sell personal information.

5. Storage and Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Actual storage, access controls, and retention practices depend on the deployment and configuration of the service.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for issue management, legitimate operational needs, or applicable requirements. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, contact us using the email below. We may need to verify your request before acting on it.

7. Third-Party Services

WhatsApp and Meta services are subject to their own terms and privacy policies. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable policies.

8. Children's Privacy

If the service is used by children, appropriate safeguards and any legally required permissions should be in place. Contact us with concerns about information submitted by a child.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
